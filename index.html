The Artist

A life given to the stage.

Rohit Jain is a dedicated and versatile theatre artist, educator, director and singer who has contributed to the performing arts for years.

Teaching, directing and performing are one continuous act of storytelling.

Since 2017, he has taught drama at the Artists Combine Institute of Performing Arts, nurturing the creative and technical growth of emerging talent.

In 2022, he earned his Master of Performing Arts (MPA) from Raja Mansingh Tomar Music and Arts University, Gwalior, securing first position in his class and three Gold Medals for academic and artistic excellence.

He has qualified the UGC-NET (National Eligibility Test), reflecting his subject expertise and commitment to arts education.

He has also compiled and edited books for UGC-NET Performing Arts / Theatre examination preparation.
